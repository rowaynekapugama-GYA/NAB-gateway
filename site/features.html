Features

Take card payments on your invoices without a third-party processor.

If you use Xero and bank with NAB, you already have everything you need. Transiqe connects the two and looks after the payment, the reminders and the bookkeeping that follows.

Pay by card, straight from your Xero invoice

A Pay now button is attached to the invoices you send from Xero. Your customer clicks it, sees exactly what is due and pays by card. The payment runs through your own NAB Gateway.

  • Works on the invoices you already send
  • The amount always comes from the invoice
  • Card fields provided by NAB Gateway

Your NAB account, your NAB rate

You no longer need a separate payment processor to take a card payment. You transact directly through your NAB Gateway account and pay the fees you agreed with NAB as a NAB customer. Transiqe adds nothing to them.

What stays with NAB

  • The card fees, as agreed in your merchant agreement
  • The money, which settles to your NAB account
  • The relationship, including your statements and support

Transiqe is software. It is never in possession of your funds.

Autopay your customers control

Customers can save a card and opt in to automatic payments, and opt out again whenever they like. They choose when their card is charged and get a receipt every time.

  • Opt in while paying, or from the portal
  • Opt out at any time
  • Clear terms shown before they agree

Set up a repeating invoice in Xero and every invoice it creates flows into Transiqe. Customers on autopay are charged automatically. Everyone else receives the invoice with a Pay now button.

A portal for every customer

Each of your customers gets a portal where they can see their invoices, pay what is due, download receipts and manage their saved card. It carries your business name, not ours.

  • No password to remember
  • Every open and paid invoice in one place

Reconciled in Xero

Every card payment is recorded against its invoice in Xero as it happens, with the payment reference. Your invoices show as paid and your books match what lands in your NAB account.

  • Invoices marked paid automatically
  • One dashboard for paid, due and on autopay

Your customers come with you

Once you connect Xero, all your customers are brought into Transiqe automatically, along with their open invoices. There is nothing to re-enter, and new customers you add in Xero appear on their own.

No Xero? Invoice from Transiqe

If you don't use Xero, you can create and send invoices straight from Transiqe. Your customers get the same Pay now button, portal and autopay, connected to your NAB Gateway.
