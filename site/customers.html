One tap from the email

The invoice email has a Pay by card button. The pay page shows the invoice, the amount, and one button. No account needed.

Card entered on the bank's page

The card form is NAB's, not ours and not yours. If they save the card, the bank keeps it and we keep a reference, never the number.

Autopay they can switch off

Customers choose whether to save a card and when it's charged. They can change the card or turn autopay off from the portal at any time, and every charge sends a receipt.

No surcharge, no surprises

The amount on the invoice is the amount charged. Card surcharges on eftpos, Visa and Mastercard are banned in Australia and Transiqe never adds one.

The portal, when they want the full picture

Every open invoice, every receipt, saved cards and autopay settings. Sign in is a link emailed to them, valid once, so there's no password to look after.
