Fair to your customers

Automatic payments, on their terms.

  • Autopay is always opt-in

    A customer is only ever charged automatically if they have chosen it. They see plain-language terms first, including what will be charged and when. Read the autopay terms.

  • They can opt out at any time

    From their portal, in a couple of clicks, or by asking you. Opting out stops future automatic payments straight away.

  • They are told about every payment

    Customers receive every invoice and a receipt after each payment, so they always know what was charged and why.

  • Their card details are handled by NAB

    Card details are entered into fields provided by NAB Gateway. Transiqe does not store full card numbers or security codes. See security.
