What to ask NAB about a NAB Gateway account

A short checklist for the conversation with your NAB business banker, so your account is set up for invoices and autopay from day one.

25 November 2026 · 4 min read

To take card payments through Transiqe, you need a NAB Gateway account: the facility NAB provides for taking card payments online. If you do not have one yet, your NAB business banker can set it up. These are the questions worth asking so it suits invoicing and autopay from the start.

About the facility

  • Is it set up for online payments? Invoice payments are made online, without the card in front of you, so the facility needs to allow that.
  • Which cards will it accept? Ask which card types are enabled, and whether American Express is included or arranged separately.
  • What are the fees? Ask for the rate, any monthly fee, and how fees are charged to you. These are between you and NAB, and Transiqe adds nothing to them.
  • Where and when does it settle? Confirm which account payments settle to and how long settlement takes.

About saved cards and autopay

  • Can cards be stored for future payments? Autopay needs the facility to save a customer’s card securely as a token, so future invoices can be charged without the card details.
  • Are refunds enabled? You will want to be able to refund from your Transiqe dashboard, as well as manually in NAB Gateway.
  • Is extra authentication required? Ask whether customers will be asked to confirm some payments with their bank, and when.

About connecting Transiqe

  • Can I generate API keys? Transiqe connects using a REST shared secret key, which you generate in NAB Gateway under Payment Configuration and Key Management.
  • Who do I call if something stops working? Note your NAB Gateway support contact alongside your merchant ID.

What to have ready

Your ABN and business details, a description of what you sell and how you invoice, and your website if you have one. Once your NAB Gateway account is live, connecting it to Transiqe takes a couple of minutes. The setup guide walks you through it.
