What do I need to use Transiqe?

A NAB Gateway account, which NAB provides to its business customers for taking card payments online, and ideally a Xero subscription. If you don't use Xero, you can send invoices from Transiqe itself.

How is this different from the payment option I use now?

Most payment add-ons are separate processors. They take the payment, charge their own fee and pay you out later. Transiqe is software only. The payment runs through your own NAB Gateway and settles to your NAB account, at the fees you agreed with NAB.

Does Transiqe hold my money?

No. Card payments go from your customer to your NAB merchant facility and settle to your account. Transiqe moves the information: the invoice to your customer and the payment record back to Xero.

What happens with repeating invoices?

Set them up in Xero as you do now. Each invoice Xero creates flows into Transiqe. Customers who have opted in to autopay are charged automatically, and everyone else gets the invoice with a Pay now button.

Can my customers opt out of automatic payments?

Yes, at any time, from their portal or by asking you. Autopay is always the customer's choice and they see the terms before they opt in.

What changes in Xero?

Very little. Your invoices gain a Pay now button, and payments appear against invoices as they are made. Your bookkeeper will see them recorded with the payment reference.
