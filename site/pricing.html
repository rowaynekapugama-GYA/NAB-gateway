What your bank charges, for comparison

NAB Transact, settling to a NAB business account, is currently $30 a month for up to $2,000 of card turnover, then 1.5% per transaction above that, with same-day settlement for transactions before 9:30pm. These are NAB's published rates and may change; confirm them with NAB when you apply. There is no Transiqe margin on top.

Getting set up, start to finish

Day 1Create your account and connect Xero. Your contacts and open invoices appear in the dashboard. Nothing is emailed to customers yet.
Day 1 to 10Apply for NAB Transact if you don't have it. NAB sends a merchant ID and passwords. Paste them into Transiqe; we verify the connection on the spot.
Same dayPaste the Pay now link into Xero's payment services and assign it to your invoice theme. Send a test invoice to yourself and pay it with NAB's test card.
OngoingInvoices go out with a card button, customers switch on autopay, payments land in Xero. You check the dashboard when you feel like it.
