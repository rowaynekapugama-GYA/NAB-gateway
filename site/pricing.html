What you pay NAB

Card payments are processed on your own NAB merchant facility. The fees are the ones in your agreement with NAB, and NAB charges them to you directly. They are not part of your Transiqe subscription, and Transiqe adds nothing to them.

What you pay Transiqe

One flat monthly subscription for the software, based on how many invoices you send. No setup fee, no per-payment fee and no percentage of your takings.

Pricing questions

The short answers.

Are card fees included in the plan price?

No. Card fees are charged by NAB under your own merchant agreement, at the rate you agreed with NAB. Your Transiqe plan covers the software only.

Does Transiqe take a percentage of each payment?

No. There is no percentage and no per-payment fee from Transiqe. You pay the flat monthly price for your plan.

Do I need to apply or wait for approval?

Not from Transiqe. Sign up, choose your plan and connect Xero and your NAB Gateway. If you do not have a NAB Gateway account yet, that is arranged with NAB.

What if I send more than 1,000 invoices a month?

Talk to us at hello@transiqe.com and we will price a plan around your volume.
