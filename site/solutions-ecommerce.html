Questions

Common questions.

See all questions

Does Transiqe work with my online store’s checkout?

Not yet. Today Transiqe handles the orders you invoice from Xero or from Transiqe. Checkout for online stores through your own NAB Gateway is on the way.

Can trade customers pay on terms?

Yes. The invoice carries its due date from Xero. Customers can pay any time before then, or opt in to autopay so their card is charged on the due date.

Are card fees different for large orders?

Card fees are set by your merchant agreement with NAB. Transiqe adds nothing to them, whatever the size of the order.
