Getting started

What is Transiqe?

Transiqe is software that connects your Xero invoices to your own NAB Gateway account. Your invoices get a Pay now button, your customers pay by card, the money settles to your NAB account, and the payment is recorded back in Xero.

What do I need to use Transiqe?

A NAB Gateway account, which NAB provides to its business customers for taking card payments online, and a Xero subscription. If you do not use Xero, you can send invoices from Transiqe itself.

How long does setup take?

Under five minutes if you have your NAB Gateway details to hand. Sign up, connect Xero, connect your NAB Gateway and switch on Pay now. The setup guide walks through each step.

Do I need to apply or wait for approval?

Not from Transiqe. There is no application and no approval wait. You do need an active NAB Gateway account, which is arranged with NAB.

I do not have a NAB Gateway account yet. What should I do?

Speak to your NAB business banker. Our article What to ask NAB about a NAB Gateway account lists the questions worth asking.

Payments and fees

How is this different from the payment option I use now?

Most payment add-ons are separate processors. They take the payment, charge their own fee and pay you out later. Transiqe is software only. The payment runs through your own NAB Gateway and settles to your NAB account, at the fees you agreed with NAB.

What does it cost to take a card payment?

Card fees are the ones in your merchant agreement with NAB, and NAB charges them to you directly. Transiqe adds no percentage and no per-payment fee. You pay Transiqe a flat monthly subscription for the software.

Does Transiqe hold my money?

No. Card payments go from your customer to your NAB merchant facility and settle to your account. Transiqe moves the information: the invoice to your customer and the payment record back to Xero.

When does the money reach my account?

On NAB’s settlement timetable under your merchant agreement. Transiqe is not in the money flow, so it does not hold or delay settlement.

Which cards can my customers use?

The card types your NAB Gateway facility is set up to accept. Check with NAB if you are not sure which ones are enabled.

Will my customers pay a surcharge?

Not from Transiqe. Customers pay the amount on the invoice, and Transiqe never adds a card surcharge.

How do refunds work?

Refund a payment from your Transiqe dashboard and the money goes back to your customer’s card through your NAB Gateway.

Autopay and repeating invoices

What is autopay?

Autopay lets your customer save their card and have your future invoices paid automatically, on the timing they choose. It is always the customer’s choice and they see the autopay terms before they agree.

Can customers turn autopay off?

Yes, at any time, from their portal or by asking you. Turning it off stops future automatic payments straight away.

Is autopay the same as a direct debit?

No. Autopay is a recurring card payment. A direct debit takes money from a bank account. They have different rules. Our article Autopay and direct debit are not the same thing explains the difference.

How do repeating invoices work?

Set them up in Xero as you do now. Each invoice Xero creates flows into Transiqe. Customers on autopay are charged automatically, and everyone else gets the invoice with a Pay now button.

What happens if a card is declined?

Your customer is told straight away and can pay with another card. For autopay, Transiqe lets them know, tries again, and tells you both if it still cannot collect. The invoice stays open in Xero the whole time.

Xero

What does Transiqe do in my Xero account?

It reads your contacts and invoices and records payments against invoices. It does not create, edit or delete your invoices.

Are my customers imported?

Yes. When you connect Xero, your customers and their open invoices are brought into Transiqe automatically, and new ones keep coming across as you add them.

How do I add the Pay now button to my invoices?

Add Transiqe as a payment service on your invoice branding theme in Xero. The Pay now guide shows where.

I do not use Xero. Can I still use Transiqe?

Yes. You can create and send invoices straight from Transiqe. Your customers get the same Pay now button, portal and autopay.

Your customers

Do my customers need an account?

No. They pay from the invoice. If they want to see past invoices or manage autopay, they can sign in to their portal with a link sent to their email.

What is the customer portal?

A page for each of your customers, under your business name, where they can see open and paid invoices, pay what is due, download receipts and manage their saved card and autopay.

Will my customers see the Transiqe name?

Your invoices, emails and the portal carry your business name. The web address of the payment page and its small print show that it is provided by Transiqe.

Security and privacy

Does Transiqe store card numbers?

No. Card details are entered into fields provided by NAB Gateway. Transiqe keeps only the token NAB issues for saved cards, plus the card brand, last four digits and expiry. See security.

How is personal information handled?

Under the Privacy Act 1988 and the Australian Privacy Principles. Our privacy policy sets out what we collect, why, and who we share it with.

How do I report a security issue?

Email security@transiqe.com with enough detail for us to reproduce it.

Plans and billing

How much does Transiqe cost?

Starter is $49.95 a month for up to 100 invoices. Growth is $99 a month for up to 1,000 invoices. If you send more than 1,000 a month, talk to us. See pricing.

Are card fees included in my plan?

No. Card fees are charged by NAB under your merchant agreement. Your plan covers the Transiqe software only.

Can I cancel?

Yes, at any time. Cancellation takes effect at the end of the billing period you have paid for. Your invoices in Xero and your merchant facility with NAB are not affected.
