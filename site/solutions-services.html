Questions

Common questions.

See all questions

Can different clients pay at different times?

Yes. Each client chooses whether to use autopay and when their card is charged: when the invoice is issued, on the due date, or a set number of days before.

What if a client prefers to pay by bank transfer?

They still can. The Pay now button is an extra option on your invoice. Payments made any other way are recorded in Xero as they are today.

Do my clients need an account?

No. They pay from the invoice. If they want to see past invoices or manage autopay, they can sign in to their portal with a link sent to their email.
