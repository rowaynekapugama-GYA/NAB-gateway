Getting started

Create your Transiqe account
  1. Go to app.transiqe.com/signup.
  2. Enter your name, your business name and your work email, and choose a password.
  3. Choose your plan. You land on your dashboard with a short setup checklist.
Connect Xero
  1. On your dashboard, select Connect Xero.
  2. Sign in to Xero and choose the organisation you invoice from.
  3. Approve the connection. Transiqe can read contacts and invoices and record payments. It does not create, edit or delete invoices.
  4. Your customers and open invoices are imported automatically. New ones keep coming across as you add them in Xero.
Find your NAB Gateway details
  1. Sign in to your NAB Gateway account.
  2. Note your merchant ID. It is shown at the top of the page as your Transacting ID.
  3. Go to Payment Configuration, then Key Management.
  4. Select Generate key and choose a REST shared secret key.
  5. Copy the key ID and the shared secret. The shared secret is only shown once, so keep it somewhere safe until you have added it to Transiqe.

If your NAB Gateway screens look different, your NAB contact can point you to your API keys.

Connect your NAB Gateway
  1. In Transiqe, go to Settings, then NAB Gateway.
  2. Enter your merchant ID, key ID and shared secret.
  3. Select Save. Transiqe checks the connection with NAB and shows Connected when it works.
Switch on Pay now in Xero
  1. In Transiqe, go to Settings and copy your Pay now link.
  2. In Xero, open your invoice settings and go to Payment services.
  3. Add a payment service, choose Custom payment URL, and paste your Pay now link.
  4. Apply it to the invoice branding themes you use. Invoices sent with those themes now carry a Pay now button.
Send yourself a test invoice
  1. In Xero, create an invoice for a small amount to yourself and approve it.
  2. Open the invoice from the email and select Pay now.
  3. Pay with your own card. The payment appears in your Transiqe dashboard and the invoice is marked paid in Xero.
  4. Refund it from the dashboard if you like. See refunding a payment.

Getting paid

How your customer pays an invoice
  1. Your customer opens the invoice email and selects Pay now.
  2. They see your business name, the invoice and the amount due.
  3. They enter their card into fields provided by NAB Gateway and confirm.
  4. They get a receipt, the money settles to your NAB account, and the invoice is marked paid in Xero.
Payment reminders
  1. Transiqe sends reminders for overdue invoices automatically, each with the Pay now button.
  2. To send an invoice again yourself, open it in Invoices and select Email pay link.
  3. Every email sent to a customer is listed in your account, so you can see what went out and when.
Refunding a payment
  1. Go to Payments and find the payment.
  2. Select Refund and confirm.
  3. The money goes back to the customer’s card through your NAB Gateway.
  4. Record the refund in Xero as you normally would.
When a card is declined
  1. If a customer’s card is declined while paying, they are told straight away and can try another card.
  2. If an autopay charge is declined, the customer is emailed and the payment is tried again.
  3. If it still cannot be collected, automatic attempts stop and you are both told. The invoice stays open in Xero.

Autopay

How customers turn on autopay
  1. When paying an invoice, your customer ticks the option to save their card and pay future invoices automatically.
  2. Or they sign in to their portal and go to Autopay and cards.
  3. They choose when their card is charged and accept the autopay terms.
Turning autopay off for a customer
  1. Go to Customers and open the customer.
  2. Under Autopay, turn it off and save.
  3. Any scheduled charges for that customer are cancelled. Customers can also turn it off themselves in their portal.
Repeating invoices with autopay
  1. Set up the repeating invoice in Xero as usual.
  2. Each invoice Xero creates flows into Transiqe automatically.
  3. Customers on autopay are charged on the timing they chose. Everyone else receives the invoice with a Pay now button.

Your customers

Inviting a customer to their portal
  1. Go to Customers and open the customer.
  2. Select Send portal invite. They receive an email with a secure sign-in link.
  3. Customers can also request a new link at any time from the portal sign-in page.
Updating a customer’s email address
  1. Update the contact in Xero and the change comes across to Transiqe.
  2. Or open the customer in Transiqe, change the email and save. This does not change Xero.

Account and billing

Replacing your NAB Gateway keys
  1. In NAB Gateway, generate a new REST shared secret key as described in finding your NAB Gateway details.
  2. In Transiqe, go to Settings, then NAB Gateway, and select Replace keys.
  3. Enter the new key ID and shared secret and save. Replace your keys straight away if you think they may have been exposed.
Disconnecting Xero
  1. In Transiqe, go to Settings and select Disconnect next to Xero. You can also remove the connection from inside Xero.
  2. Your customers and invoices stay in Transiqe, but new invoices stop coming across until you connect again.
Cancelling your subscription
  1. Email hello@transiqe.com from the address on your account, or cancel from your account settings.
  2. Cancellation takes effect at the end of the billing period you have paid for.
  3. Scheduled autopay charges stop. Your invoices in Xero and your merchant facility with NAB are not affected.
