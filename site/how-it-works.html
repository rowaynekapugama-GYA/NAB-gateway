How it works

From Xero invoice to money in your NAB account, step by step.

You set Transiqe up once. After that, every invoice you send from Xero can be paid by card through your own NAB Gateway, and the payment is recorded back in Xero for you.

3

Connect your NAB Gateway

Link the NAB Gateway account NAB has set up for your business. From then on, card payments on your invoices run through your own merchant facility and settle to your NAB account.

What is NAB Gateway?

It is the online card payment facility NAB provides to its business customers. If you bank with NAB and do not have it yet, your NAB business banker can set it up.

  • Your merchant agreement with NAB
  • The card fees you agreed with NAB
  • Settlement to your NAB account

They see the invoice and the amount due and enter their card. The card fields are provided by NAB Gateway and connected to your account, so the payment goes straight to your merchant facility.

  • The amount always comes from the invoice
  • They can save the card for next time

Part three

Repeating invoices and autopay.

1

Set up the repeating invoice in Xero

Use repeating invoices in Xero as you normally would for retainers, memberships or regular services. Each invoice Xero creates flows into Transiqe automatically.

2

Your customer opts in to autopay

When they pay an invoice, or from their portal, your customer can choose to save their card and have future invoices paid automatically. It is their choice, they see the terms first, and they can opt out at any time.

  • Customers opt in and out themselves
  • A receipt is sent after every payment
  • Customers not on autopay simply get Pay now

The less usual days

When something doesn't go to plan.

  • A card is declined

    Your customer is told straight away and can pay with another card. For autopay, Transiqe lets them know, tries again, and tells you both if it still cannot collect. The invoice stays open in Xero the whole time.

  • An invoice is overdue

    Transiqe sends payment reminders for you, each with the Pay now button, so chasing does not depend on you remembering.

  • You need to refund a payment

    Refund it from your Transiqe dashboard, or manually in your NAB Gateway if you prefer. Either way the money goes back to your customer's card through your own merchant facility.

  • You void or change an invoice in Xero

    Transiqe follows Xero. If an invoice is voided or already paid another way, any scheduled autopay for it is cancelled.
